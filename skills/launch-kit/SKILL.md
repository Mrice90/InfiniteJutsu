# Launch and store kit

Existing: launch kit Claude Doc, EULA and privacy drafts, SteamPipe scripts on 3DTuba branch `claude/launch-kit-rcr6r2` (`releases/steam/steampipe.py stage/render/upload`; preview by default, refuses to set the default branch live).

## Steps
1. Read the current scope first: Zeus vs Poseidon as the full 1.0, other gods as expansions.
2. Write the kit as a Claude Doc with tabs: overview and "Needs Matt", store copy, capsule art and trailer specs, press kit, pricing and name check, age rating, platform plan.
3. Research store rules live (Steam fee and 6+ week lead time, in-game purchases must use Steam's own payment).
4. Check the name for conflicts (stores, trademark databases); recommend a lawyer's clearance, never give a legal opinion.
5. Draft EULA and privacy policy marked "for legal review".
6. Keep an AI disclosure ledger of every generated asset (provider, job id) for the store's AI content form.
7. List open decisions for Matt in one place: store, pricing model, name, card art provenance.

Uploads and store changes wait for Matt's explicit go.
